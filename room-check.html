Overview

What matters to you? The weights decide the score and the order. Click a column heading to sort by that criterion.

What matters

WaysPeople come in at the entrance and cross to the window or balcony door. A clear way of about 90 cm and a piece of open floor make a room easier to use and let it feel larger.
DoorsEvery door should open all the way, and from the bed and the desk you want to see who comes in. A bed straight in line with a door gets the draught and the view from outside.
DaylightAt a desk, daylight from the side is ideal. Looking straight at a window dazzles, and with the window behind you it reflects in the screen.
ZonesSleep in the darker, quieter part of the room, work and live in the bright part near the windows. Where one piece of furniture does both jobs, the zones mix.
ScreensA TV or a projected picture needs a clear view from the seats: nothing tall in front of it, whether furniture, a column or a wall block.

Feng Shui

An optional way of reading the same plan. It adds its own terms and never changes a score.

Commanding positionBed and desk stand with a solid wall behind them and the door in sight, but not straight in its line. Seeing the door is said to help you rest and concentrate.
ChiEnergy enters at the door and moves through the room. A free, gently curving way and an open centre let it spread, while a straight line from the door rushes at whatever stands there.
Yin and yangThe quiet, darker part of the room is yin and suits rest. The bright, active part is yang and suits work and living. A place that does both mixes them.
